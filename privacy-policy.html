Privacy Policy

Your information is handled carefully, kept confidential, and never sold.

This page explains what information is collected when you use this website, how it's used, and how it's protected. If anything here is unclear, feel free to reach out directly at therafay804@gmail.com.

Your Data Is Confidential

Any information you share — your name, email, requirements, budget, or project details — is used only to understand and deliver your order. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

What Information Is Collected

  • Your name and email address, if you create an account or submit a quote request
  • Project details, budget, and deadline information you choose to share via the Get a Quote form
  • Reviews and ratings you submit voluntarily after an order is completed
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, used only to make the site work correctly

How Your Information Is Stored

Account details and order history are stored securely so that you can log in from any device and see your own information. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect this data from unauthorized access.

Partner Freelancers

If your order is completed with the help of a trusted partner freelancer, only the specific project details needed to complete the work are shared with them. Your payment information, password, and personal contact details are never shared with anyone outside of what's needed to deliver your order.

Payment Information

Card numbers, bank passwords, or wallet PINs are never requested or stored on this website. Payment details are exchanged directly and securely over WhatsApp when confirming an order — the same way you'd arrange payment with any trusted local seller.

Your Rights

  • You can request a copy of the information stored about you at any time
  • You can request your account and order history be deleted
  • You can ask questions about how your data is used before submitting any information

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the site or its features. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact therafay804@gmail.com or message directly on WhatsApp.
